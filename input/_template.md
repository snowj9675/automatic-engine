# Lesson brief

Rename this file to match its id, for example cooking-with-mama-a1.md, and change
the id to cooking-with-mama-a1. Replace these two sample rows with the complete
8–10 minute dialogue. Use exactly two people. Keep each A1 row to at most 12
German words (A2: 18). Each row has the speaker name, German and English text.
The names correspond to left_name and right_name. Voice defaults are Thorsten
on the left and Eva on the right. These settings and prompts are data, not code.

## Image prompt

Warm original illustrated home kitchen. Exactly two people: adult son Ben on the
LEFT and his mother on the RIGHT. They prepare vegetable soup at a kitchen table.
Clear faces, consistent clothes and warm natural light. Landscape 16:9, no text,
no speech bubbles, no watermark. Leave some sky/window or wall space above the
faces for dialogue overlays. Save the image at the path in the settings.

Set image_ready: true only after uploading the image. The workflow skips this
lesson while image_ready is false; it does not generate missing images itself.
