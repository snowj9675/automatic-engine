# Scene image prompt

Create a polished, warm illustrated scene for a beginner German conversation.
Exactly two people: a mother seated on the RIGHT and her adult son on the LEFT.
They are at a kitchen table, preparing a simple vegetable soup. Show both faces
clearly, angled toward each other. Include a pot, carrots, potatoes and two bowls.
Warm natural window light, welcoming home, consistent character proportions,
clean expressive faces, cinematic colours. Landscape 16:9 image, at least
1280×720. No text, captions, watermarks or speech bubbles. Keep both people fully
visible within the image. The pipeline adds the dialogue and translations.

Save the chosen image here as `background.png`. Keep `ready: false` until it exists.

# Thumbnail image idea and prompt

Use the same two characters, kitchen, clothing and art style. They smile beside a
colourful bowl of soup. Show the food clearly; simple uncluttered composition.
Landscape 16:9, expressive faces, no generated text. Leave room near the bottom
for the title “Kochen mit Mama”. Generate and save as `thumbnail.png` if you want
a custom cover; otherwise the pipeline creates one from the background image.

If providing a custom thumbnail, add `"thumbnail": "thumbnail.png"` under `assets`
in episode.json. A custom thumbnail is copied as supplied; the pipeline does not
add its own title on top of it. Add title text yourself before saving if desired.

# Lesson brief for the script writer

Level: A1. Two speakers only. Use everyday present-tense language about washing,
cutting, cooking and eating. Keep one caption to at most 12 German words. Include
accurate English translations. Build a natural sequence with small changes in the
situation; avoid repeating the same exchange just to reach a duration target.
Aim for 8–10 minutes at the selected voices, speed and pauses, then review the
measured production report. For A2, change the level and introduce appropriate
past tense, reasons and simple plans, with captions of at most 18 German words.
